# Participles

- There are 3 types of participles.

1. **Present Participles**: `verb + ing`
2. **Past Participles**: `verb3`
   They are especially used in two sections.
   **Reducing Relative Clauses**: when the clause begins with `who, that, which, or whom + a verb`, then participles can be used to abbreviate the sentence with below rules: 1. If the sentence is active => Form 1 : - `The girl who is singing is my sister.` => `The girl singing is my sister.` 2. If the sentence is passive => Form 2: - `The book that was written by her won an award.` => `The book written by her won an award.` 3. If the sentence is passive, but we want to emphasize the time => having been v3 - `The vaccine which is being developed by WHO is expected to deeply affect our lives.` => `The vaccine having developed ...`

**Reducing sentences,conjunctions**: If two sentences have the same subject, we can remove the subject from the first sentence and use a participle phrase instead.

     - `This medicine is known to be rich in vitamins.It is used by millions of people all over the world.`
     - `Known to be rich in vitamins, it is used by millions of people all over the world.`
     - `While Ece was asked whether she would go to Italy,She said that she might.` => `While asked whether she would ...`
     - `If this medicine is consumed with alcohol, it may lead to poisoning.` => If consumed with alcohol,...

- `Having written tens of articles on obesity, Professor Adams is a reputable expert.` => `Having written tens of articles on obesity, Professor Adams is a reputable expert.`
- Additional Forms

1. `being+ verb3` => `to show ongoing passive action`: `The documents being signed right now are important.`
2. `having + verb3` => `to show completed action before another`: `Having finished the report, she left the office.`
3. `having+ been+verb3`=> `to show completed action before another for passive sentences` : `The report was prepared by the assistants last week. It was presented to the board of managers.` => `Having been prepared by the assistants last week, the report was presented to the board of managers.`

`While going on holiday last summer, it rained all day.` // ❌ subjects are not the same.
`While we are going on holiday last summer,It rained all day.` // ✅

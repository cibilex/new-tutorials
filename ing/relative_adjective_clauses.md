# relative (adjective) clauses


| Pronoun   | Refers to      | Function           | Example                                   | Note                                               |
| --------- | -------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **who**   | People         | Subject            | The teacher **who teaches us** is kind.   | Can be replaced with **he/she/it** (subject) .In speaking, “who” is often used for both subject and object.     |
| **whom**  | People         | Object (formal)    | The girl **whom I met** was friendly.     | Can be replaced with **him/her/it** (object)       |
| **whose** | People/things  | Possession         | The man **whose car broke down** is here. | Shows **possession** (his/her/its)                 |
| **which** | Animals/things | Subject/Object     | The movie **which we saw** was boring.    | Used for **things or animals**                     |
| **that**  | People/things  | Subject/Object     | The book **that I borrowed** is lost.     | Can replace **who/whom/which** in defining clauses |
| **where** | Places         | Refers to location | The school **where I studied** is famous. | Refers to **place**                                |
| **when**  | Time           | Refers to time     | 1995 was the year **when we moved here**. | Refers to **time**                                 |
| **why**   | Reason         | Refers to reason   | I don't know the reason **why she left**. | Refers to **reason**                               |

1. `which/that` vs `where`:
   - `My school ... was built in 1991 is a nice school.` . This relative clause describes the place itself (the school as an object). `The school was built in 1991.`.If the relative clause starts with verb ,always use `which/that`
   - `My school .... they built in 1991 is a nice school.`. `They built the school| it in 1991.`  We must use which/that.
   - `My school ... I graduated last year is a nice school.`. `I graduated from the school | from there last year.`  we should use the `where`.
2. `which/that` vs `where`: 
   - `I can't see her on Friday, ... is the busiest time of the year for me.` => ` Friday is the busiest time of the year for me` // We must usee which/that
   - `I can't see her on Friday, ... many people find gloomy.`  => `Many people find it (Friday) gloomy.` // We must use which/that
   - `I can't see her on Friday, ... everybody is away for winter holiday.` => ` Everybody is away for winter holiday at that time.` // we must use when
3. `when` ,`where` with prepositions: We can  replace “when” or “where” with “preposition + which” in more formal or written English.However, we should not always use "in which" for where, or "on which" for when — the preposition must match the verb or context of the sentence
   - `The university at which I studied is famous.` // where
   - `Friday, on which we have a meeting, is fully booked.` // when
4. **Defining** vs **non-defining**: If the relative clause gives extra information or talks about a unique/specific person or thing, then it is a non-defining relative clause.
    - `Atatürk, who commanded our army in the Independence War, is a great leader.`  Non-defining (comma is correct) 
    - `My neighbor who will be here in ten minutes is my best friend.`  Defining (no comma)
    - `My sister, who can speak English very fluently,is writing a new book` // He has a only one sister.!!
    -  **That** cannot be used after commas.
5. **which**: To give more information about the thing mentioned
    - `He helped me to complete my homework, which saved my job.` => `Because he helped me with my homework, I was able to keep my job.`

**Reducting relative clauses**
1. If the relative clause is subject relative clause or whom. (which,who,whom,that)
   1. If the sentence is active =>Remove relative clause,remove auxiliary verb,add+ing to the main verb. `ving` | `having v3`
     - `Police  is taking the license of those who violate the traffic rules repeatedly` => those violating the traffic rules ...
   2. If the sentence is passive=>remove relative clause,remove auxiliary verb and use past participle form . `v3` | `having been v3`
     - ` The giver,which was written by lois lowry, is a distopian novel.` => the giver,written by lois
2.  if the verb is "HAVE" we can change it with "with"  
  - `Ayşe is dating a man who has three children` => having three children or with three children
3. If the there is superlative,order(the first,the last),the only or modal(can,might,could) prev the relative clause
    1. If the sentence is active=> `to+verb` | `to have v3`
       - `Last Monday,I was the last person who left the work.` =>the last person to leave the work
    2. If the sentence is passive=>`to+be+v3` | `to have been v3`
       - `There must be a lot of books which/that were written  on this topic.` => a lot of books to be writted
4. Reductionlarda soru çözerken passive cümlelerde ilk kural dışındahi hepsi (be+verb3) alır.Aktif cümlelerde ise (be+verb3) dışındaki seçeneklere bakılır.
       - Bu iki kural dışındakilerdeki şıklar çeviri farklılığıdır.
       - `The turks,.... to anatolia in 1071,still live the same geopgraphy.` migrating or having migrated => göç eden,göç etmiş olan.
       - Başka seçenekler var ise mantık aynı dır. Yani `verbing` şıklarda yoksa `having verb3` bakılır.`verb3` şıklarda yoksa `having been verb3` bakılır.`to be verb3` yoksa `to have been verb3` bakılır

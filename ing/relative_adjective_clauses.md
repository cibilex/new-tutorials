# relative (adjective) clauses




**Notes**:
1. **relative clause as subject** if the blank followed by a verb,the blank can be which,that or who.     
    - `London,... is capital of england,attracts million tourists every year.`
2. **relative clause as object** if the blank followed by subject+verb and relative clause des not have an object(kimi,neyi,what)  ,the blank can be `who/that/which/whom`
    - `Murat who/that/whom you invited to your birthday party is the manager of the language course.`
3. **relative clauses with prepositions**:If blank followed by a preposition,the blank can be `which/whom/whose` . svo yapısı sever
   - `The girl ... he danced with at the party yesterday is his girlfriend.` who/that/whom
   - `The girl with ... he danced at the party yesterday is his girlfriend.` whom
   - `The bus  ... the passengers have been travelling on is extremely old.` which/that
   - `The bus on ... the passengers have been travelling is extrelemy old.` which
   - `Billie ellish is the women with whose cousin I am dating.` 
4. **possesive clauses**: Noun+....+noun =>whose ,of which
   - `Turkey,whose population is at around 80 million these days,is located at the mediterranean region.`
   - `This is the computer whose properties I explained to the customer.`
   - `Billie ellish is the women whose cousin I am dating with`
5. **of which**: the noun of which.  noun+...+noun
    - `This is the computer the properties of which I explained to the customer.`
6. **where**: ...+ subject+verb+ has object(neyi,kimi) or object+verb3 . where is not equal with in which
    - `There is a cafê  on the first floor of the liblary where you can get coffee if you feel asleep`
7. If the sentence finishes with a preposition(in,on,for).We should be aware that there is a lack of object.So we must back to the rule 2 
   - `We usually don't go into that room ... my sister studying for the SAT` where
   - `We usually don't go into that room,... my sister studying for the SAT in` which)(that cannot used after comma)
8. ...+ subject+verb+object or passive sentence(be+verb3) if we want to emphasize the time.
   - `2015 is the year when I graduatedfrom university.`
9.  that cannot be used in non-defined sentences(after commas).This rule is for just non-defining relative clauses.(extra information)
10. If we want to refer to whole the sentence `which` must be used.
     - `I got the lowest score in the class,which really suprised me.`
11. whereby,by which,through which,by means of which =>yoluyla,yöntemiyle,vasıtasıyla
     - `Multiple option questions are the most used way .... the achievement level of  the students are measured.`

**Reducting relative clauses**
1. If the relative clause is subject relative clause or whom. (which,who,whom,that)
   1. If the sentence is active =>Remove relative clause,remove auxiliary verb,add+ing to the main verb. `ving` | `having v3`
     - `Police  is taking the license of those who violate the traffic rules repeatedly` => those violating the traffic rules ...
   2. If the sentence is passive=>remove relative clause,remove auxiliary verb and use past participle form . `v3` | `having been v3`
     - ` The giver,which was written by lois lowry, is a distopian novel.` => the giver,written by lois
2.  if the verb is "HAVE" we can change it with "with"  
  - `Ayşe is dating a man who has three children` => having three children or with three children
3. If the there is superlative,order(the first,the last),the only or modal(can,might,could) prev the relative clause
    1. If the sentence is active=> `to+verb` | `to have v3`
       - `Last Monday,I was the last person who left the work.` =>the last person to leave the work
    2. If the sentence is passive=>`to+be+v3` | `to have been v3`
       - `There must be a lot of books which/that were written  on this topic.` => a lot of books to be writted
4. Reductionlarda soru çözerken passive cümlelerde ilk kural dışındahi hepsi (be+verb3) alır.Aktif cümlelerde ise (be+verb3) dışındaki seçeneklere bakılır.
       - Bu iki kural dışındakilerdeki şıklar çeviri farklılığıdır.
       - `The turks,.... to anatolia in 1071,still live the same geopgraphy.` migrating or having migrated => göç eden,göç etmiş olan.
       - Başka seçenekler var ise mantık aynı dır. Yani `verbing` şıklarda yoksa `having verb3` bakılır.`verb3` şıklarda yoksa `having been verb3` bakılır.`to be verb3` yoksa `to have been verb3` bakılır

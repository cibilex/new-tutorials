    # Would, Would prefer , Would rather

**would rather,'d rather** **than**
1. can used for present or past. vanilla verb. Use with `than` or `rather than`
2. used for comparisons
3. Cannot used with nouns
4. Can not used with nouns. `I would rather hot chocolate` is wrong.
5. used to make some requests or complaint.
6. If two subjects exists in the sentence,the structure `Subject+would rather+subject2+past simple`
7. For regrets
    1. If objects are the same: `subject+would rather+have+verb3`
    2. If objects are different: `subject+would rather+had+verb3`

- I would rather go  school than by bus to by my motorcycle.
- I’d rather you treated me kindly.
- I’d rather  have eaten meat at this restaurant than the other.
- I’d rather you had brought me an expensive gift than this trash.

**prefer** **to**
1. gerund | infinitive | noun ,
  
- I prefer drinking tea than milk in the mornings.
- I prefer hot chocolate to cold one
- I prefer to sleep early.

**would prefer**
1. infinitive | noun
  
- I would prefer to cook alone.
- I would prefer you to cook me.
- I’d prefer tea to coffee.

# would vs used to

**woud**
1. repeated actions

- I would go to the store every afternoon.
- They would pay me a visit everyday when I was rich.

**used to**
- can be used instead of "would" but "would" cannot...
- +habit
- I used to go to the store every afternoon.
- I used to play on the school team.


**get used to**
1. to become something normal

- I have gotten used to cats.
- I have gotten used to sleeping early on weekends.
- Will you get used to the new school ?

**be used to**
- I am used to cats.
- I am used to the new school.
- I am used to sleeping early on weekends.

**Note** : used to > emphasize that I underwent   an adaptation while usually is a generic habit.

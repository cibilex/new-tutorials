# Causative

**active cousative** : to make someone to do someting

1. **_have_**: to pay,to request `I had the waiter bring me a cup of cofee`
2. **make**: to entail,to force,to order `I make my little brother make his bad`
3. **let** : to let,to allow `I let my sister go outside`
4. **get**: to convince,to pursuade `I got my sister to make lunch`
   asdasdas
   **passive causative**: agent is optional. `subject+causative verb+object +verb3`

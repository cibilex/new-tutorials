# Notion Notes

Word-choice nuances, collocations and structures collected from Notion.

## Using something for benefit

- **take advantage of** — positive or negative depending on context; can be opportunistic.
  - *We should take advantage of the low interest rates to buy a new house.*
- **tap into** — positive only; information, experience, potential.
  - *She knows how to tap into her creativity.*
- **utilize** — "to use", formal and robotic.
  - *We need to utilize our resources efficiently.*

## be made up of / make up / be made of / comprise

- **be made up of** → focuses on the whole (bütüne odaklanır)
  - *The team is made up of...* (Takım nedir?)
- **make up** → focuses on the parts (parçalara odaklanır)
  - *Ten players make up...* (Kimler oluşturuyor?)
- **be made of** → the material of something
  - *This building is made of wood.*
- **comprise** → focuses on the whole
  - *The committee comprises five members.*

## pioneer

- **pioneer + object** (not `to + verb`)
  - *He pioneered many innovations in science.*

## Inversion

Professional, serious or emphatic. Usually used with negatives or restrictions.

- With restrictive phrases (*only*, *only after*), the first part stays **normal** but the
  main clause **flips** into a question-like structure.
- *Not only **does this invention** decrease the costs, but also improves the productivity.*
- *Under no circumstances **should you** share any information with our competitors.*
- *Only after you finish your work **can you** go out.*
- ***Rarely do I** wake up this early.*

## No sooner … than …

"olur olmaz", "yapar yapmaz".

- *I had no sooner finished the report than my boss asked for another one.*
- *No sooner had I finished the report than my boss asked for another one.*

## by + numbers

Means "with too many".

- *by the hundreds* → yüzlerce
- *Planets are being discovered around other stars by the thousands.*

## Countable / uncountable

- Uncountable: information, merchandise, weather, feedback, hay, evidence
  - *The company disclosed the confidential information to the public.*

## Collocations: prevent / deter

- **prevent** someone/something **from** doing something
  - *The international community has called for restraint to prevent instability from rising in the region.*
- **deter** someone **from** something
  - *He tried to deter me from buying that house.*

## commit

- **commit to + object | gerund** — dedicate, promise.
  - *He **committed to** finishing the project on time.*
  - *I'm sure that the team will **commit to** this project easily.*
  - *He **committed himself to** his career.*
- **commit something** — to perform, carry out. Negative tone.
  - *The officers are still investigating who **committed the** crime.*
- **commit changes** — to save changes.
  - *Please don't forget to **commit your changes**.*
- **commit suicide** — to die by suicide.
  - *Unfortunately he **committed suicide** yesterday.*

## sense of + human quality

- **sense of humor** → mizah anlayışı
- **sense of responsibility** → sorumluluk duygusu
- **sense of direction** → yön duygusu / yön bulma yetisi
- **sense of justice** → adalet duygusu

## edge / border / boundary

- **edge** — the outline limit of something such as a table, cliff, ocean.
  - *Don't put your glass on the edge of the table, it might fall.*
- **border** — geographical or political line separating two countries or regions.
  - *The border between the two countries has been a topic of debate for years.*
- **boundary** — allowable space or limit.
  - *You should maintain the professional boundaries with your boss.*

## look forward to

**look forward to + object / gerund**

- *I'm looking forward to meeting you this weekend.*
- *I'm looking forward to the new project.*

## confess / admit

- **confess / confession** — illegal or heavy.
  - *The officer couldn't get a confession from the murderer yet.*
  - *Finally she confessed that she stole the money.*
- **admit / admission** — neutral facts or argument.
  - *Why does everybody expect an admission from me?*
  - *I admit that you are better than me in swimming.*

## Emotion structures

- **in + emotion** → state
  - *He slammed the door in anger.* ⇒ He was angry.
- **adverb (-ly)** → manner (how)
  - *He slammed the door angrily.*
- **out of + emotion** → cause (why)
  - *He slammed the door out of anger.* ⇒ because he was angry.

## Gestures + at

- *She winked at me in the meeting.*
- *Why are you staring at me?*
- *He smiled at me.*

## sting / bite

- **bee, scorpion** → sting
  - *A scorpion **stung** me.*
- **mosquito, insect, spider** → bite
  - *A spider bit my leg.*

## stick / be stuck / get stuck

- **stick** — stay in memory, endure.
  - *This question stuck with me for years.*
- **be stuck** — state, condition.
  - *I'm stuck with this task.*
- **get stuck** — end up with something unwanted; event, physical.
  - *I got stuck in traffic.*
  - *I got stuck with a huge bill.*

## Replacing a previous verb or action

- **do so** — formal.
  - *You should complete this task until 08:00 PM.* — *I'll do so.*
- **do it** — casual.
  - *I'll do it.*
  - *Why don't you clean up your room? I told you to do it **many times**.*

## compliment / praise / brag

- **compliment / give a compliment** — casual and polite, saying something nice about someone.
  - *Thanks for your compliment.*
  - *He complimented me on my eye color.*
- **praise** — formal and strong, showing approval or admiration.
  - *The governor praised the teacher for his work.*
- **brag** — to talk proudly about your own achievements or abilities, often annoyingly.
  - *Why do you always brag about yourself?*

## If it weren't for …

**If it weren't for … would / could**

- *If it weren't for my parents, I couldn't afford college.*

## the + official title

*Mehmet the Conqueror*, *Alexander the Great* — we know them by this adjective.

## "about" / "-e gelince"

- **about, -e hakkında**: regarding, as regards, with regard to, with respect to, as to, concerning
  - *I am worried **as to** the project deadline.*
  - *What do you think regarding leaving university?*
- **-e gelince**: when it comes to, as for
  - *We should do our tasks on time. When it comes to new requirements, we can postpone them for now.*
- **uyumlu**: conforming
  - *We only merge **conforming** pull requests into the `main` branch.*

## be tied up

- **be tied up** — busy.
  - *I couldn't answer because I was tied up.*
- **be tied up with** — busy, occupied doing something.
  - *My boss is tied up in a meeting. I'll inform you when he is available.*
- **be tied up in** — be involved in a situation or activity.
  - *I'll be tied up with this new feature for a while.*

## -free

**free** — without, not containing, not affected by.

- sugar-free, gluten-free
- stress-free, meeting-free
- crime-free, tax-free

## sahipsiz

- **things**: abandoned, unclaimed
  - *I found this abandoned car on the sidewalk.*
- **animals**: stray
  - *We should find a home for this stray cat.*
- **people**: orphan
  - *We want to establish a charity for orphans.*

## get + past participle

More dynamic than the passive.

- Instead of the passive, for unusual activities:
  - *My window was broken* = *My window got broken.*
  - *He was arrested* = *He got arrested.*
- The action happens to the subject:
  - *I got invited.*
  - *I got fired.*

## recommend

- **gerund**
  - *The doctor recommended consuming more soy products.*
- **that + someone + base verb**
  - *The doctor recommended that he consume more soy products.*
- ~~*The doctor recommended him to consume more soy products.*~~
- *The doctor advised him to consume more soy products.* ✅

## bark / shell

**bark** for plants, **shell** for animals.

- *The bark protects the tree from damage.*

## emotion + to + infinitive

Explains the reason for the emotion.

- *I am happy to see you.*
- *She was shattered to learn the truth.*

## hang up

- **hang up** — to hang something onto something.
  - *Please hang up your coat there.*
- **be hung up (on)** — to dwell on something, to be obsessed.
  - *I am hung up on this problem.*

## Money amounts

- `$20` → *twenty dollars*
- `$12.40` → *twelve dollars and forty cents* / *twelve forty*
- `$0.10` → *ten cents*

## fee / fare / fine / bill

- **fee** — money paid for a professional service or platform usage.
  - *The lawyer sent us a $200 fee for his two hours of consultation.*
- **fare** — money paid to travel.
  - *How much is the bus fare from the airport to downtown?*
- **fine** — money paid as a penalty for breaking a rule.
  - *I got a $50 fine yesterday because I parked my car in front of a school.*
- **bill** — a digital or printed statement showing money owed for goods/services used.
  - *Don't forget to pay the electricity bill before the end of the month.*

## reject / refuse / decline

- **reject** — officially say "no" because something is not good enough.
  - *The company rejected my application.*
- **refuse** — strong, direct way to say no.
  - *He refused our recommendation.*
- **decline** — polite way to say no.
  - *He declined our invitation.*

## make + it + adjective

- **make + it + adjective + for someone + to do something**
  - *The goal of my application is to make it easier for people to learn English.*
- **make + noun + adjective + for someone**
  - *Our teacher makes the class interesting and fun for us.*

## remind

**remind someone of something / someone**

- *Your laugh reminds me of my mother.*
- *His speech reminds her of her ex.*

## cry / weep / sob / wail

- **cry** — generic.
  - *The girl is crying.*
- **weep** — quiet, emotional.
  - *He has been weeping for a long time because he couldn't pass the exam.*
- **sob** — loudly, strong.
  - *The baby is sobbing and his mother isn't doing anything about it.*
- **wail** — loudly, intense.
  - *The mother has been wailing for a long time because she lost her son in the war.*

## key to

- *Tenacity is key to success.*
- ~~*Tenacity is key for success.*~~

## no matter

- *No matter where you go, I'll find you.*
- *No matter how difficult the project is, we will complete it on time.*
- *No matter the weather, the game goes on.*

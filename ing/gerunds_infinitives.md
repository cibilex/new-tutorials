**Can used with both**
- begin
- continue
- prefer
- start
- cease
- hate
  

1. ***remember***: 
   * to remember something from the past. `I remember going to school on my birthday.`
   * to remember responsibilities.`I finally remembered to pay my bills.`
2. **forget**:
   * to forget something: `I forgot saying his name.`
   * to forget responsibilities: `I forgot to lock the door.`
3. **regret**:
   * to be sorry to doing something: `I regret yelling at him for nothing.`
   * to be upset to do someting:`I regret to inform you that you haven't been selected.`
4. **try**:
   * to try an alternative `I am trying riding this new vehicle.`
   * to try to do something,to make effort to do it `I tried to be a doctor.`
5. **stop**:
   * to stop doing something `will you stop talking?`
   * to stop to do someting `We stopped to buy some groceries.`
6. **go on**:
   * to carry on with something not done yet `you should go on doing your homework.`
   * to carry on with something new `they went on to dance  on the stage.`
7. **propose**:
   * to suggest ,to recommend  `I propose sleeping early for your healhy.`
   * to plan to do someting `We are proposing to buy a new car.`
8. **like,love**: 
   * to like,love doing something `I like making my bad` `I love watching tv`
   * to want  `I don't like to go the cinema` `I love to be there`
9. **need**:
    * `This house needs cleanig.`
    * `we need to obey the rules all the time.`

* Prepositions are used with gerunds.
    - `We are looking forward to seeing your beautiful face`
    - `Did you talk about cleaning the house?`

* possesive adjectives are used with gerunds.  
    - `Hear leaving home made me crazy.`

* have fun,have a trouble,have a good time ... are used with gerunds.
    - `We had fun playing soccer with the children.`
  
* busy,worth are used with gerunds.
    - `I was busy preparing the lunch.`
    - `It is worth replacing this phone with a new one.`
* go+ sport activity is used with gerunds.
    - `I am going dancing with my girlfriend.`
    - `I am going playing chess this afternoon.`
* verb+object+infinitive
    - exceptions:
        - `spend/waste+object+gerund`
        - `help+object%verb0 | infinitive`
        - `algı/şahirlik+object+verb0 | infinitive`
        - `have+object+v0 |verb3`
        - `get+object+to verb0 | verb3`
        - `soyut isimler(tendency,urge)+infinitive`
    - `Our teacher adviced us to study harder.`
    - difficult+`infitinive` but difficulty+`gerund`
* sit,stand,lie +place adverb+  are used with gerunds.
    - `She was lying on the couch reading a novel.`
    - `I am standing there doing nothing.`
* find,catch + personal pronouns+ gerund
    - `I found him kissing your sister`
    - `I caught her stealing your eraser.`
* phrasal verbs can be used with both gerund and infinitives.It depends the pharasal verb itself.
    - `He chickened out of jumping from the crevice.`
    - `He got him round to sign the contract.`
    - `When will you finish off gathering the crop?`
* how +adjective+gerunds
    - `how beautiful  seeing you`
* see,hear,listen
    - from zero to end of the process ` He saw me play soccer.`
    - from middle of the process `he saw me playing soccer.`
* Causative sentences used with vanilla verb:  
    - I made him paint the wall.
* It takes + infinitive `It took botanists  years to breed`
- seem,appear + to lu yapılar gelir. (to be getting,to be verb3,to be)
* pasif cümlelerde istisnalar dışında to verb0 alır.
     - `catch,see,heard` +gerund
     - `find`=>... yaparken ise gerund,.... olduğu,yaptığı keşfedildi farkedildi to verb0   
* ,verb ... => ask questions verbs to determine  whether is transitive or intransitive.`neyi,kimi,kime,neye`>cavap yoksa intransitive varsa transitive o zaman object olmalı>obje ara varsa aktif yoksa pasif cümle.
    - if the sentence is active  : `gerund` 
    - if the sentence is passive : `verb3`
* adjective ... => ask questions verbs to determine  whether is transitive or intransitive.`neyi,kimi,kime,neye`>cavap yoksa intransitive varsa transitive o zaman object olmalı>obje ara varsa aktif yoksa pasif cümle.
    - if the sentence is active  : `infinitive` 
    - if the sentence is passive : `to be verb3`
Other examples:
   - `They suggested me to rent a car.`
   - `It is nice to see you here.` adjective+infinitive
   - `I am about to leave` be about to+infinitive

# Prepositions


- **in**
1. if the location have certain edges like box,container,country
    - `I live in Ankara.`
2.  talking about liquids 
    - `Do you prefer swimming in the sea or in  a swimming pool ?` 
    - `in the sea`=>swimming `on the sea`=>on a boat
3. Finally,eventually= in the end 
    - `I tried to get to him; in the end, I reached him at 09:00.`
4. Go,been,went,done always are always used with `to`.
    - `I am going to Ankara.`
    - `Have youe ever been to Ankara?` is true while `been in Ankara` is wrong.
5. Months,years,morning,evening,afternoon.
     - `I play chess with my friends in the evenings.`
     - `I like swimming in summer.`
6. car,taxi
     - `will you get in the car?`
7. Bilim alanında anlamını in verir.
      - `in computer science` `in Math`
- **on**
1. If location is over or hand a thing
    - `The key is on the table` 
    - `The photo is on the wall.`
2. week days,special days,specific days,weekends
    - `I will go to the Ankara on the 25th April.`
    - `We will get together on Christmas.`
3. bus,boat,bike,horse
    - `What are you doing on the horse?`
4. internet platforms,applications
    - `I watched a horror movie on netflix.`
    - `This video exists on my channel.`
- **at**
1. at a starting,ending point.
    - `I am waiting at the end of the street.`
    - `I am waiting in the middle of the street.` !!! middle is used to desribe middle point
2. specific places:
    - `I saw you at the party`
    - `I live at 5 Grocer Street.`
    - `we are having lunch at the table`
    - `I put the dishes on the table.`
3. specific time,night,midnight,noon.
    - `I will play soccer at noon.`
    - `I woke up at 09:15`


- **above,over**:
  1. over is refers to movement
    -  `The plaine flies over our house.`
  2. above: higher location
    - `There is a bird above my house.`
    - `your score is above mine.`
  3. If there is touching : on otherwise above
    - `My nose is above my mouth`
    - `My pencil is on the table` 

- **under-below**
  1. **under-underneath** covering,movement
    - `Your legs are under the table.` 
  2. **below-belowneath** lower location
    `My mouth is below my nose.`
  3. beneath=under
- **of**:
    - `the rooms of the house`
    - `both of the apples`
    - `a bottle of milk`
- **by**:
  1. shows agent
    - `I got my hair cut by my dad.`
  2. shows instrument
    - `I will go to school by bus.`
  3. desribe the method
    - `Can I pay by  credit card?`
    - `I will keep in touch by letter.`
  4. during,until
    - `I will be graduated by the end of this year.`
    - `I will be studying by the summer.`
  5. up to
    - `I will increase my score by 20.`
  6. location
    - `I live by beach`
- **into**
   1. to show a movement to in or inside a object.
      - `Greg put his guaitar into his case.`
- **at length=for a long time**
    - We discussed the problem at length.
